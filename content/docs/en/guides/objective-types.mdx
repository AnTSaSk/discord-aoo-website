Black Zone objectives in Albion Online are timed open-world events that spawn valuable resources. Albion Online Objectives (AOO) tracks five categories: **resource nodes**, **energy cores**, **vortexes**, **Ancient Lands Portals**, and **Dragons**.

This page lists every type and rarity supported by the [`/add`](/docs/commands/add/) command.

## Resource Nodes

Resource nodes spawn gatherable materials in the Black Zone. Each node type corresponds to a crafting resource. Nodes use **tier-based rarities** from <Badge variant="tier">4.4</Badge> to <Badge variant="tier">8.4</Badge>; higher tiers yield rarer materials.

## Vortexes

## Ancient Lands Portals

Ancient Lands Portals are timed portals that come in **three sizes** instead of a rarity: <Badge variant="tier">Small</Badge> <Badge variant="tier">Medium</Badge> <Badge variant="tier">Large</Badge>. The size is picked in the `rarity` field of `/add` and determines the in-game group capacity.

<ObjectiveCard
  name="Ancient Lands Portal"

  description="Timed portals in three sizes; the size sets the group capacity."
/>

### Portal Sizes

| Size | Group capacity |
|------|----------------|
| **Small** | 1–5 players |
| **Medium** | 5–7 players |
| **Large** | 15–20 players |

See the [Ancient Lands Portals & Dragon attacks guide](/docs/guides/portals-and-dragons/) for time limits per size and reporting details.

## Dragons

Dragon attacks are the only objective type with **no rarity at all**: the attack's tier follows the tier of the map it spawns on. They strike the Black Zone and the Royal Continent alike. When reporting one with `/add`, leave the `rarity` field empty; the bot rejects a Dragon report that carries a rarity.
